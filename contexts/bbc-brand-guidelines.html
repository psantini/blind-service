06
Usage guidelines
Do
Use the seal on black as the primary logo treatment
Use Cormorant Garamond italic for all headings and display text
Use amber sparingly — for accents, scores, and active states only
Use parchment (#F2ECD8) for body text on dark — not pure white
Maintain the lockup ratio — seal and wordmark at consistent proportions
Use the BBC monogram when the full seal won't fit (under 48px)
Don't
Don't place the seal on busy or coloured photo backgrounds
Don't use the wordmark without the italic serif — no bold sans substitute
Don't use amber as a background colour for large areas — accent only
Don't add drop shadows, glows, or other effects to the seal
Don't stretch or distort the seal — always scale proportionally
Don't use pure white (#fff) for body text on dark — use parchment
